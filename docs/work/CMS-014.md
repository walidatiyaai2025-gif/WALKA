## Four delivered regression tasks

1. **Backend propagation regression**
   - exercises owner Dashboard HTTP routes for governed Product presentation, PDP layout and Related Products;
   - verifies the resulting public `/api/v1/catalog`, `/api/v1/content/pdp-layout` and `/api/v1/content/related-products` contracts;
   - snapshots Product facts, internal sort order, variant ASIN, Pantone and internal sort before the edit and proves they are unchanged afterward.

2. **Flutter content-state propagation regression**
   - loads PDP layout and Related Products together through their production repositories into `WalkaContentController`;
   - proves remote published revisions become active state;
   - proves an older remote revision cannot replace the newer last-known-good cache.

3. **Live PDP regression**
   - renders the production `WalkaDrawerProductDetailV112` under real Catalog, Content and Favorites scopes;
   - proves remote optional layout visibility is applied;
   - proves a valid Related Product card navigates through the compiled product route;
   - proves a CMS relationship cannot surface a target absent from the visible catalog snapshot.

4. **Commerce-boundary regression**
   - proves remote Related Products data is normalized to stable IDs only even if hostile URL/ASIN/purchase fields are present;
   - proves those fields cannot mutate the Amazon purchase registry;
   - proves a non-Amazon registry override is filtered and the official compiled WALKA Amazon fallback remains the destination.

## Guardrails preserved

- no new URL-authoring surface;
- no Product Master, ASIN or Pantone mutation;
- no executable navigation supplied by CMS;
- no direct database coupling from Flutter;
- Amazon handoff remains outside CMS-controlled PDP layout and Related Products;
- no production media or release gate is weakened.
